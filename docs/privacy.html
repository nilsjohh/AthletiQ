Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

AthletiQ does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your favourite teams and leagues, and whether you've completed "Your Q Profile", are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they're deleted when you delete the app.

Internet connections

To show scores, fixtures, standings and team badges, AthletiQ downloads publicly available sports data and images directly from the providers' servers:

  • Scores, schedules, standings and statistics from public sports data feeds (ESPN).
  • Team and league badges from TheSportsDB.

As with any website or app that loads content, these servers receive standard technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the data. AthletiQ doesn't send them anything about you, and we have no access to that information.

Children

AthletiQ doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
