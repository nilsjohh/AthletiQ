How do I choose my teams and leagues?

Tap the profile button at the top of Home to open "Your Q Profile". Pick your leagues, then your teams. They appear at the top of Home.

What is Gameday?

When one of your teams plays today, a Gameday banner appears on Home with the countdown, live score and key stats. Tap it for the full match centre with lineups.

Scores aren't updating

Check your internet connection. Live games refresh automatically about every 30 seconds while the app is open.

A team shows initials instead of a badge

Some teams don't have a badge available yet. They're shown with a monogram in the team's colours instead.
